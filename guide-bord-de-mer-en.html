Seaside day trips with a private chauffeur

Fancy a day by the sea without driving or hunting for parking? From Paris, your private chauffeur takes you to the finest coasts of Normandy and the Opal Coast, at a fixed price and door-to-door.

Fixed one-way fare from €350 in an Eco sedan (€550 Premium, €750 Van) for every destination below — return trips and tailor-made days also available.

Our seaside destinations

  • ✦
    Le Havre. Port and post-war centre by Auguste Perret, a UNESCO World Heritage site; seafront and pebble beach.
  • ✦
    Deauville. The chic Normandy resort: the Boardwalk, Belle Époque villas, casino and racecourse.
  • ✦
    Honfleur. Picturesque old harbour, half-timbered houses, cradle of the Impressionist painters.
  • ✦
    Étretat. Spectacular alabaster cliffs and natural arches, panoramic clifftop gardens.
  • ✦
    Fécamp. Fishing port, the Bénédictine Palace and high chalk cliffs.
  • ✦
    Saint-Valery-en-Caux. A small seaside resort nestled between two cliffs, with a marina.
  • ✦
    Dieppe. France's oldest seaside resort, a castle-museum and a long seafront.
  • ✦
    Le Tréport. Among the highest cliffs on the coast, a funicular, harbour and seafood.
  • ✦
    Le Crotoy. Bay of the Somme: the region's only south-facing beach, seals and a little steam train.
  • ✦
    Le Touquet-Paris-Plage. The "Pearl of the Opal Coast": golf, forest, a vast beach and Art Deco villas.
